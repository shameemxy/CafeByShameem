03 / Quick answers

Small rules.

Short answers to the things people ask before their first good cup.

How should I store my beans?

Keep them in an airtight container, away from light, heat and moisture. Buy amounts you can finish within a few weeks of the roast date, since flavour fades over time.

Arabica or robusta?

Arabica is generally sweeter and more aromatic, with more acidity. Robusta is usually more bitter and heavier-bodied, and has more caffeine. Neither is better, they are just different.

Does the water really matter?

Yes. Water makes up most of the cup. If your tap water tastes of chlorine or is very hard, filtered water will usually taste cleaner.

How hot should the water be?

For most hot methods, roughly 93 to 96 °C works well. That is just off the boil, so let the kettle sit for 30 seconds or so.

Why weigh the coffee?

Scoops vary a lot between roasts and grind sizes. A cheap scale makes every cup repeatable, which is the only way to improve on purpose.
